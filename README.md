Chat Agency Skills

Learn to direct the chat. Keep the decisions. Stop when you need to.

Chat Agency Skills is an independent educational project for people who want practical, transferable skills for using AI conversations.

It teaches learners to control their goals, context, boundaries, pace, verification, and decisions. It does not promise complete control over AI behaviour or guarantee accurate answers.

All project files are currently stored at the repository root. The links below open the files directly.

The STEER method

Step	Purpose	
S — Set	Define the outcome, audience, and finish line.	
T — Tell	Provide necessary context, constraints, and privacy boundaries.	
E — Explore	Work in small steps, ask questions, and compare options.	
E — Examine	Check facts, assumptions, sources, relevance, and safety.	
R — Redirect	Correct, reset, record, pause, or stop.	

Stopping is treated as a successful choice—not a failure.

Independence from AOE3

Chat Agency Skills is not part of AOE3’s technical architecture.

- Chat Agency teaches human judgement, communication, verification, privacy, and stopping.
- AOE3 addresses technical authorization, execution boundaries, evidence, and consequential actions.

MK AOE3 Academy may optionally distribute an approved version as a separately attributed educational module. Academy adoption does not authorize installation, modification, publication, or technical integration.

Safety and privacy

Do not submit passwords, identification documents, exact locations, school details, health records, private conversations, or another person’s information.

These materials provide education—not therapy, crisis counselling, or professional advice. Important claims should be checked independently. Consequential decisions require appropriate human support and qualified advice.

Read [Safety and responsible use](SAFETY.md) before group or classroom use.
