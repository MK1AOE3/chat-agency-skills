# Safety and responsible use

## Educational scope

These materials teach communication, verification, privacy, and personal boundaries. They do not provide therapy, crisis counselling, or professional advice. AI output can be wrong, inappropriate, or unexpectedly persuasive even when a learner follows every exercise.

## Privacy by default

- Use fictional details in public examples and classroom activities.
- Do not share passwords, exact location, school details, identification numbers, private documents, health records, or another person's information.
- Check attachments, screenshots, code, and document metadata before sharing.
- The repository does not need learner accounts, chat logs, or progress data. Keep any personal playbook locally unless you deliberately choose to share a sanitised version.

## Difficult or overwhelming chats

Stopping is a valid skill. If a chat becomes confusing, pressured, compulsive, or uncomfortable, stop generating content, step away, and contact a trusted person if support would help. Do not ask learners to describe traumatic experiences in a public setting.

If anyone may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or an appropriate local crisis service and a trusted person now. AI chat is not sufficient emergency support.

## Learners and families

Follow current platform age requirements and school rules. Parent–teen exercises should be consensual and age appropriate. They are not a basis for covert monitoring or demands to reveal every private conversation. Younger children require closer adult supervision.

## Consequential decisions

Verify important information using authoritative sources. For medical, legal, financial, safety-critical, or similarly consequential matters, seek qualified human advice. Never submit fabricated citations, analysis, code, or other work as verified.

## Reporting a repository safety issue

Do not open a public issue containing personal data or a dangerous prompt example. Use the repository's private reporting channel once configured by the maintainer. Until then, report only a minimal description without sensitive content.
