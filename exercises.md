# Guided exercises

Use any AI chat for the prompting exercises, or rehearse with another person. Choose low-risk, fictional material. Do not upload private chats or personal information.

## Exercise 1: Turn a vague request into a finish line

**Weak request:** “Help me study.”

**Your task:** Write a result, audience, time limit, and observable completion criterion.

**Example:** “Create a five-day chemistry revision plan with 30 minutes per day, one active-recall task per session, and a final self-test.”

**Pass when:** another person can tell whether the result is complete without asking what you meant.

**STEER skill:** Set.

## Exercise 2: Remove unnecessary personal data

**Your task:** Write a fictional request containing a person's name, school, exact location, and a private concern. Redact everything the task does not need. Explain why each remaining detail matters.

**Pass when:** the request still works but cannot identify a real person.

**STEER skill:** Tell.

## Exercise 3: Slow down an oversized answer

**Your task:** Ask for a complete project, then deliberately replace that request with an outline plus one first step. Compare the control you have in each approach.

**Pass when:** you can approve or change the direction before substantial work is generated.

**STEER skill:** Explore.

## Exercise 4: Find the unverified claim

**Your task:** Ask for an explanation of a familiar topic. Label each important statement as checked, suggestion, or needs checking. Verify one factual claim against a reliable source.

**Pass when:** you identify at least one assumption or limitation and independently check one claim.

**STEER skill:** Examine.

## Exercise 5: Redirect without starting over

**Your task:** Give feedback using three parts: what to keep, what to change, and what not to alter. Avoid “make it better” without a criterion.

**Pass when:** the next response follows your specific correction and preserves the useful part. If it does not, identify the failure rather than assuming you caused it.

**STEER skill:** Redirect.

## Exercise 6: Stop and reset deliberately

**Your task:** During a low-risk practice chat, use the pause card. Step away for two minutes. Decide whether to continue, start fresh, or leave the task unfinished.

**Pass when:** you make the decision yourself and can state why.

**STEER skill:** Redirect, reset, or stop.
