# Path: ages 16–20

## Focus

Build independence without making AI your decision-maker. This path connects study, creative work, early career tasks, and everyday planning while protecting privacy and emotional pace.

## Five short practices

### 1. Define your own finish line

Choose a real but low-risk task. Write the result, audience, and constraints before opening a chat. Ask for questions before answers.

**Success looks like:** you can tell whether the response met your goal without relying on “it sounds good.”

### 2. Practise a privacy edit

Take a fictional prompt containing a name, school, address, and private problem. Remove or replace details that are unnecessary. Compare the original and redacted prompts.

**Success looks like:** the task remains clear while identifying details are gone.

### 3. Keep choices yours

Use this prompt:

> Give me three options with benefits, costs, and unanswered questions. Do not choose for me or tell me what I should value.

Pick an option yourself, or reject all three. For major life, relationship, health, legal, or financial decisions, involve appropriate people and qualified support.

**Success looks like:** you can explain why you chose, not merely what the AI recommended.

### 4. Challenge confident language

Ask the AI to distinguish facts, assumptions, and suggestions. Independently check one important claim using a reliable source.

**Success looks like:** you can name at least one thing the chat did not establish as fact.

### 5. Use a stop-and-reset boundary

Practise saying:

> Pause. Do not add more advice. Summarise the task status in two lines so I can decide whether to return later.

Step away. Return only if you have a clear purpose. If a chat leaves you distressed, pressured, or unable to stop, contact a trusted person; immediate danger requires local emergency or crisis support, not more AI chat.

**Success looks like:** you can leave without finishing the AI's last suggestion.

## Transfer challenge

Reuse STEER in two different areas—for example, revision and a creative project. Keep the same five steps but change the finish line and verification method.

## Keep in your playbook

- My default time limit for a chat.
- Details I never share.
- Topics for which I contact a person first.
- My best redirect prompt.
- My personal stop signal.
