# Path: high school

## Focus

Use AI to strengthen learning while following current teacher and school rules. The goal is not to produce the most polished submission; it is to increase what you can understand, explain, and do yourself.

## Four classroom-safe habits

### 1. Ask for hints before answers

> I want to solve this myself. Give me one hint and ask me to try the next step. Do not show the final answer yet.

After your attempt, ask for feedback on the reasoning rather than only the result.

### 2. Turn notes into self-testing

> Using only the notes I provide, create five short-answer questions. Wait for my answers, then identify one misconception at a time.

Check the notes for private information before sharing. If the notes are copyrighted or restricted, follow the applicable rules.

### 3. Verify research leads

AI may suggest useful search terms, but generated citations can be false. Open each source yourself, confirm its author, date, and claims, and use the citation style your teacher requires.

> Suggest search terms and questions I should use to evaluate sources. Do not create citations or claim you have read sources you cannot access.

### 4. Improve your own draft

> Identify one unclear sentence and one unsupported claim in my draft. Ask me how I want to revise them. Do not rewrite the whole assignment.

Disclose AI assistance when required. Never use these exercises to evade an assessment's rules.

## Practice sequence

1. Set a learning goal rather than a submission goal.
2. Request one hint and make your own attempt.
3. Check one explanation against class materials.
4. Correct the AI if it uses a method you have not learned.
5. Close the chat and explain the idea aloud without help.

## Success criteria

You are ready to transfer the skill when you can:

- state what kind of AI help is allowed for the task;
- solve a similar problem without the chat;
- identify and verify an unsupported claim;
- show your own reasoning and any required disclosure.

## When to involve an adult

Ask a teacher, parent, guardian, or another trusted adult when content feels unsafe, someone requests private information, a chat becomes distressing, or you are unsure whether AI use is allowed.
