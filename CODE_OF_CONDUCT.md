# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Make this project welcoming to learners of different ages, backgrounds, abilities, languages, education levels, and experiences with AI.

## Expected behaviour

- Be patient, specific, and respectful.
- Teach agency rather than dependence on a person, prompt, or platform.
- Protect privacy and use fictional or properly anonymised examples.
- Accept correction and focus on the learning value of contributions.
- Respect a person's decision to pause, leave, or decline an activity.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, humiliation, discrimination, or sexualised content involving learners.
- Requesting or publishing private chats or personal information.
- Shaming someone for a difficult experience with AI.
- Presenting AI as a therapist, unquestionable authority, or replacement for human support.
- Circumventing school rules, platform age rules, or appropriate adult supervision.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a concern through the repository's private security or maintainer contact channel when one is configured. Do not post sensitive details in a public issue. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful material, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action to protect learners and the community.
