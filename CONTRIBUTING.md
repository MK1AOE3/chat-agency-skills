# Contributing

Thank you for helping people build healthier, more effective AI-chat habits.

## Good first contributions

- Improve wording, readability, accessibility, or translations.
- Add a short exercise with observable success criteria.
- Test an activity and describe what learners found confusing.
- Add examples from a new subject without making the method platform-specific.
- Review safety, privacy, and academic-integrity guidance.

## Contribution rules

1. Use fictional, synthetic, or fully anonymised examples.
2. Never upload private chat logs or personal information.
3. Do not promise that a prompt guarantees model behaviour or factual accuracy.
4. Keep exercises short, adaptable, and focused on learner decisions.
5. For school activities, support learning and applicable AI-use rules.
6. For parent–teen activities, favour consent and collaboration over surveillance.
7. Do not add medical, mental-health, legal, or financial claims that require professional review.
8. Test every prompt pattern in at least two different task types before proposing it as reusable.

## Propose a change

Open an issue describing the learner problem, the proposed change, and the intended audience. For a pull request, explain what you tested and list any limitations. Keep changes small enough to review.

## New learning path checklist

A new path should state:

- who it serves;
- what learners will be able to do;
- prerequisites and adult or teacher involvement, if any;
- privacy and safety boundaries;
- three to five short practices;
- observable success criteria;
- a transfer activity in a different project.
