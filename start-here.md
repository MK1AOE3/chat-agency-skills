# Start here

## What you will learn

By the end of the starter session, you should be able to:

- define what a useful chat must produce;
- give enough context without oversharing;
- reduce a large request to one manageable step;
- check an answer instead of accepting confidence as evidence;
- redirect the chat or deliberately stop;
- reuse the same method in another project.

## One rule before prompting

**AI can assist your thinking; it does not own your decisions.** A polished answer is not automatically a true, safe, or useful answer.

## Your 20-minute starter session

Use a low-risk task, such as planning a study session or improving a short fictional paragraph. Do not begin with a personal crisis or consequential decision.

### 1. Set — 3 minutes

Complete this sentence:

> I want to create [result] for [audience]. I will know it is useful when [observable finish line].

Example:

> I want a seven-day revision plan for biology. It is useful when each day has one topic, one active-recall task, and no more than 40 minutes of work.

### 2. Tell — 3 minutes

Add only the context that changes the result:

- constraints: time, length, level, tools, or format;
- preferences: tone, examples, or order;
- boundaries: information not to request and tasks not to complete for you.

Remove names, exact locations, account details, and private information that the task does not need.

### 3. Explore — 4 minutes

Ask for an outline, questions, or options before a full answer:

> Give me two possible approaches and their trade-offs. Then recommend one first step. Do not produce the full result yet.

Choose the direction yourself. If you do not understand an option, ask for a simpler explanation.

### 4. Examine — 5 minutes

Mark each part of the answer as:

- **Checked:** you verified it independently.
- **Reasonable suggestion:** useful, but a choice rather than a fact.
- **Needs checking:** a factual claim, source, calculation, or assumption.
- **Not useful:** irrelevant or misaligned with your finish line.

Ask:

> Which assumptions did you make, and what is most likely to be wrong or missing? Suggest how I can check it. Do not invent sources.

### 5. Redirect — 5 minutes

Correct one specific issue:

> Keep the seven-day structure. Reduce each session to 30 minutes and add a short self-test. Change only those two things.

If the chat remains confused, start fresh with your finish line and essential constraints. If you feel uncomfortable or the task is no longer useful, stop. You do not need to “win” the conversation.

## Reflect without sharing private data

Write down:

1. Which instruction most improved the answer?
2. What did you verify yourself?
3. What will you reuse in a different project?

Continue with the path nearest to your situation, then complete the exercises in `practice/`.
