# Prompt cards

Copy a card, replace the brackets, and remove anything the task does not need. Prompts guide a chat; they do not guarantee accuracy or obedience.

## 1. Define the finish line

> My goal is [result] for [audience]. A useful answer must include [criteria] and stay within [constraints]. Tell me what is missing before you begin.

## 2. Ask useful questions

> Ask me up to three questions only where the answer would materially change the result. Otherwise, state your assumptions and proceed cautiously.

## 3. Protect privacy

> Use only the fictional or redacted details in this prompt. Do not ask for names, exact locations, account details, private documents, or information about other people.

## 4. Take one small step

> Give me an outline and one first step. Do not create the full result yet. Wait for my feedback.

## 5. Compare without deciding for me

> Give me [two or three] options with benefits, costs, and unknowns. Do not make the final choice for me.

## 6. Learn by doing

> Give me one hint, then wait for my attempt. Review my reasoning before showing another step. My goal is to solve a similar problem without help.

## 7. Check the answer

> Separate facts, assumptions, and suggestions. What is most likely to be wrong or missing? How can I verify it independently? Do not invent sources.

## 8. Correct one thing

> Keep [working part]. Change [specific issue] to [new requirement]. Revise only [section or step].

## 9. Reset the task

> For this task, use these requirements from now on: [requirements]. Summarise your understanding and flag any conflict before continuing.

## 10. Pause cleanly

> Pause. Do not add recommendations. Give me a two-line summary of where we stopped.

## 11. Save a handoff

> Summarise the goal, decisions, verified facts, open questions, and next small step. Exclude private information and do not add new ideas.

## 12. Transfer the skill

> I used this approach for [project A]. Help me identify which parts transfer to [project B] and which verification methods must change. Let me make the final plan.
